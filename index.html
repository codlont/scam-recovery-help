Been scammed? Know what to do next.

A scam can leave you feeling confused, pressured, and unsure who to trust. Get practical steps for protecting yourself, preserving evidence, reporting fraud, and understanding your options.

Start with these steps

If you've just been targeted, focus on safety first.

1
Stop sending moneyDo not pay extra fees or taxes demanded by the suspected scammer.
2
Contact your providerAsk your bank, payment app, card issuer, or exchange what action is possible.
3
Secure your accountsChange exposed passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
4
Keep your evidenceSave messages, receipts, account details, dates, and transaction references.
