About this resource

Clear information for your next step after a scam.

Scam Recovery Help is an independent educational website designed to help people understand common scams, organize relevant information, find reporting resources, and take practical steps to protect themselves.

Independent information resource — not a government agency or recovery service.

Why this website exists

Discovering that you have been scammed can be confusing and stressful. People may be unsure who to contact, what information to preserve, whether they can recover their money, or how to prevent further loss.

This website brings together general educational guidance and links to relevant resources in one place. Its aim is to make possible next steps easier to understand without promising a particular outcome.

Whether you are researching a suspicious message, documenting an incident, securing an account, or looking for an official reporting channel, you can use the information here as a starting point.

Who we are

Scam Recovery Help is presented as an independent information resource. It is not affiliated with a government, police force, financial institution, cryptocurrency exchange, law firm, or official fraud investigation agency.

References to public agencies and other organizations are provided to help visitors find their own official resources. Mentioning an organization does not mean that it endorses this website.

Principles that guide this website

Scam-related information should help people make informed decisions, not pressure them into risky choices.

✓

No guaranteed recovery claims

No particular financial recovery, investigation, refund, chargeback, or account restoration is promised.

✓

Encouraging official channels

Visitors are encouraged to contact relevant banks, payment providers, platforms, law enforcement, and official agencies directly.

✓

Awareness of follow-up scams

We highlight the risk of people who promise to recover lost money in exchange for upfront fees, passwords, or sensitive information.

✓

Respect for privacy

Visitors should avoid sharing passwords, authentication codes, private keys, full payment credentials, or unnecessary personal information on public pages or with unknown parties.

✓

Clear limitations

General educational material is not a substitute for case-specific legal, financial, technical, or law-enforcement advice.

✓

Informed decision-making

The purpose is to help visitors understand possible next steps without using fear, pressure, or unrealistic promises.

Important information and limitations

This website provides general educational information only. It does not investigate individual cases, trace funds, freeze accounts, contact banks on your behalf, provide legal representation, or recover money.

Recovery options depend on the circumstances, the payment method, the organizations involved, applicable laws, and how much time has passed. A bank, payment provider, platform, law-enforcement agency, or qualified professional is best placed to explain the options available in your situation.

External websites are operated by their respective organizations. Their availability, procedures, and content may change. Check that you are using the genuine official website before sharing any information.

Never pay an unknown person who guarantees recovery, asks for your password or one-time code, requests your cryptocurrency wallet's recovery phrase, or pressures you to act immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scam Recovery Help a government website?

No. It is an independent educational resource and is not an official government, police, or fraud-investigation website.

Can this website recover my lost money?

No. This website does not receive or trace funds and cannot guarantee recovery. Contact the bank, payment provider, exchange, or other organization involved as soon as possible to ask about available options.

Do I need to pay to use the information on this website?

The information presented on these pages is intended as educational guidance. Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent this website who asks for money or sensitive information.

Can I report a scam through this website?

The reporting page provides guidance and links to external resources. Unless a specific reporting function is clearly provided, this website does not submit reports to authorities or financial institutions for you.

What should I do if I have just been scammed?

Contact the relevant bank or payment provider promptly, preserve messages and transaction records, secure any affected accounts, and report the incident through an appropriate official channel. Do not send additional money to someone promising guaranteed recovery.

Is every link or resource guaranteed to be current?

No. External organizations may change their websites and procedures. Confirm current details directly with the relevant organization and verify that you are on its genuine official website.

Start with a practical next step

If you are dealing with a suspected scam, begin by preserving relevant records and contacting the organization that handled the payment or account. Use the resources here to help plan your next steps.
